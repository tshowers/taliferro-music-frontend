Install App

Before you tap install

  1. Open this page in Safari on the iPhone itself — not Chrome, not a link preview inside Messages/Mail.
  2. After it installs, the app icon will appear but won't open yet.
  3. Go to Settings → General → VPN & Device Management, tap the developer profile, and tap Trust.
  4. Now open the app from your Home Screen.
